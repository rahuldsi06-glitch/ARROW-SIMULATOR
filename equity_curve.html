Strategy Rulebook & Implementation Reference

Systematic Rules
1 The Setup (09:15 – 09:30 AM)

Let the first 15-minute candle complete. Mark its high as OR_High and its low as OR_Low. Do not take any trade before 09:30 AM.

2 The Filter (Do We Trade Today?)

Compare with Yesterday's High (PDH) and Low (PDL). If 1st candle breaks either PDH or PDL, trade today. If it stays completely inside yesterday's range: SKIP DAY (filtered 25 choppy days!).

3 The Entry & Pullback (09:30 AM – 01:30 PM)

Wait for a 15-min candle to close outside the range. Bullish: Close > OR_High. Bearish: Close < OR_Low. Don't chase. Enter only on a 50% pullback into that breakout candle.

4 Position Sizing (1% Account Risk)

Max risk is strictly 1% of account (₹10,000 on ₹10 Lakhs). Stop Loss = extreme of breakout candle. Risk $R$ = distance from Entry to SL. Size lots to ensure 1R loss = ₹10,000.
